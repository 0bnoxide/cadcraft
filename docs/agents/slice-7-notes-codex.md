## Manifest v1

The complete synthetic example is generated by `tests/fixture.rs::fixture`.
Every field is required; objects deny unknown and duplicate fields. Names are
snake_case. Top-level fields are `version`, `base_roots`, `folders`,
`model_template`, `models`, `sheets`, `sheet_index_path`, `support_records`,
`data_shortcut_path`, `profiles`, `property_mappings`, and `xrefs`.
Templates may be DXF or DWG, absolute or relative to the manifest directory.
Output parents use the exact casing of their declared folders. Input mapping
names match the snake_case fields of `Inputs`; property and attribute names
are declared explicitly. No sheet-set or DWT output is generated.
